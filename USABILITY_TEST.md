# Voluntary usability study — ready for participants

No real participant study has been completed by this release. Automated browser testing and bots only verify implementation and feasibility.

Open Play → Settings → Playtest & performance → Start voluntary usability test. The app explains that task timing and voluntary answers stay on the device. Participation is optional; do not enter personal information.

Suggested tasks, without coaching:

1. Explain the single objective before pressing Start.
2. Cut one round target and avoid one visibly spiked trap.
3. Move away from a striped warning and counter during violet recovery.
4. Find Pause; return to play.
5. On a regular campaign run, compare two power cards and explain the numerical difference.
6. Save, leave and find Continue.
7. State what caused a defeat and describe the next attempt.

Observer sheet: participant alias (optional), viewport, task success, seconds to first action, hesitation points, accidental selections, objective comprehension and motion comfort. Do not mark a test successful solely because the player wins. Record misleading cues and unclear terms verbatim, then simplify the responsible screen.

Initial practical goals: find Play within 5 seconds, understand the goal without explanation, find Pause within 5 seconds, explain at least one card's change, and correctly identify a failure cause. These are proposed evaluation targets, not measured results.

The performance view shows only actual device-local data. Obtain voluntary participation before using the study flow. A first set of 5–8 participants can identify recurring usability problems; it cannot establish broad population statistics.
