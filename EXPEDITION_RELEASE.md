# AngioBlade Expedition 6.0.0

The default Play flow now launches a dedicated mobile-first expedition with a visible drone, dodgeable enemy attacks, counter windows, real defeat conditions and 100 regenerated campaign stages across ten regions.

- 15 enemy archetypes, spiked traps, helper escort, corridors, currents, siege and alternating twin cores.
- Seeded generation, an attack-budget director, safe escape checks, optional next-room adaptation and a local tactical coach.
- Nine upgrade cards, room/reward choices, limited healing, class mastery, ten-level starting variants, region mastery cosmetics and endless waves.
- One-thumb controls, large goal/health indicators, readable warnings, bounded effects, distinct hit/crack/dodge feedback and separate comfort settings.
- Local snapshots with a protected resume countdown; isolated enemy practice does not award progression.
- Existing campaign, RPG, online rooms and records remain available through Hero / classic collections.

Implementation-to-requirement mapping: [EXPEDITION_100.md](EXPEDITION_100.md).

Validation: regression suites and expedition tests; 4,000 generated maps validated and all 100 campaign stages completed by a deterministic legal-input bot. Human difficulty tuning remains iterative; the bot is a feasibility check.
