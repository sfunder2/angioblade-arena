Key Scientific Messages:

Other anti-VEGF agents do not have the binding site for Angiopoeitin-2 (ANG-2). Faricimab is the first and remains the only approved intravitreal agent that acts as a bispecific antibody, engineered to neutralize both ANG-2 and VEGF-A simultaneously. ANG-2 inhibition preserves the blood-retina barrier by blocking vascular destabilization, preventing pericyte loss and reducing inflammation.
